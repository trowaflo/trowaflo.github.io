Privacy policy

This policy applies to the applications published by trowaflo that ask you to sign in with an account from another provider.

  • What is accessed: only the data listed on the provider's consent screen, and only after you approve it.
  • Why: to run the feature you asked for, nothing else.
  • Where it goes: it stays between the application and the provider. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • How to stop: revoke access at any time from the security settings of your account at that provider.
